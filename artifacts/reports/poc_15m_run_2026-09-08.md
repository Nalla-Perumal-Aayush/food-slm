## Sample generation (best.pt, top_k 40 / top_p 0.95)

Fluent, wikitext-register English; no repetition collapse at T≥0.9 (a little "cup cup" at
T=0.7 — a known small-model artifact).

**Structural tokens were learned from the serialized store:**

- `"In South India, "` → `…</DISH> is a rasam from Tamil Nadu.\nA thin, … staple dish in an
  area of Tamil Nadu, thin liquid, ch…` — the `_dish_doc` template (`<DISH>…</DISH> is a
  <category> from <state>.` + summary + diet trailer) reproduced verbatim in shape
- `"<DISH>"` (T=0.9) → `…4.\n1 tsp</STEP>\n4.5 tsp</QTY> Mohee…` — recipe step / quantity
  structure
- `"To make dosa batter,"` → `…pongal is a staple tamarind-smelling dish.\nHard-vegetarian
  (seafoetida).` — the `Vegetarian / Non-vegetarian (…)` trailer line; "seafoetida" = fused
  asafoetida + seafood
- Dravidian script (`த்`, `து`) emitted and round-trips through the tokenizer

**Domain knowledge ≈ 0** — "Idli is an international record for the world's national
average…", "Chicken Chettinad is the only woman to be killed…". The domain slice is
~28k tokens against 9.2M of wikitext; the model has no culinary knowledge and is not
expected to. This is a data-scale artifact, **not** a toolchain fault.

## What this validates

canonical JSONL → serializer → tokenizer → sequence packing → training loop (bf16 AdamW,
warmup+cosine, grad-accum/clip, z-loss) → checkpoint/eval/best-tracking → generation —
end to end, on real data, on the 70 W GPU. Structural tokens survive packing and are
learnable. Throughput feeds the 40M/89M/150M rung budgets.
